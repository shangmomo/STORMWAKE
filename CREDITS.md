# V63 additional geometry derivation

`public/models/stormwake_v63/dutch_ship_lower_hold.glb` derives from the bundled Poly Haven Dutch Ship Medium CC0 source `stormwake_v54/dutch_ship_lower_hold.glb`. It retains the V62 stair openings and clears four measured landing headroom volumes above the existing landing floors. Boundary positions, normals and UVs are interpolated from the original triangles. Regenerate with `tools/build_v63_stair_apertures.py`; source/output hashes and exact cut volumes are recorded in `public/models/stormwake_v63/stair_manifest_v63.json`.

`public/models/stormwake_v63/cannon_articulated.glb` derives from the bundled Kenney CC0 Pirate Kit `stormwake_v62/kenney_cannon.glb`. It separates the 140 original barrel triangles from 56 stationary carriage triangles; both original wheel/support meshes, all source vertices, UVs and the embedded palette remain intact. A pivot at the measured barrel axis and a real muzzle socket provide elevation and axial recoil. Regenerate with `python tools/build_v63_cannon.py`.

`public/models/stormwake_v63/ghost_ship_separated.glb` derives from the already bundled Quaternius CC0 Ships Pack `stormwake_v47/quaternius_ship_large.glb`. The V63 conversion separates two sewn canvas islands (308 triangles) from the opaque timber/iron hull, retaining all 20,636 original triangles, positions, normals, UVs and the embedded atlas. Reproducible conversion and measurements: `tools/build_v63_ghost_ship.py` and `public/models/stormwake_v63/ghost_ship_derivation.json`. No Sea of Thieves meshes, textures, audio or other proprietary assets are included in this change.
